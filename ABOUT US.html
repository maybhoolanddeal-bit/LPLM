WHO WE ARE

LIVE PROPERTY LIVE MONEY is an online property information and facilitation platform designed to connect property owners, buyers, sellers, clients and brokers.

OUR PURPOSE

Our purpose is to make property information and property requirements easier to share and discover through an online platform.

OUR SERVICES

  • Properties for sale and purchase
  • Residential and commercial properties
  • Agricultural land and plots
  • Client property requirements
  • Broker registration and property submissions
  • Property information and related services

OUR COMMITMENT

We encourage responsible information sharing, transparency and independent verification of property details and documents.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

LIVE PROPERTY LIVE MONEY operates as a property information and facilitation platform. It is not a property dealer or real estate agency.

Property information is supplied by contributors and should be independently verified by users. We do not guarantee property details, availability or any transaction.
